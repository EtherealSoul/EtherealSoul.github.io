LoopSong Privacy Policy

Nothing leaves your iPad

SupportPrivacy PolicyEtherealSoul

Last updated: September 19, 2026

LoopSong does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

What the app accesses

  • Microphone and audio input. LoopSong records audio so you can loop it. Recordings stay on your device in the app's own storage. They are never uploaded anywhere.
  • Bluetooth. Used only to connect Bluetooth MIDI controllers such as footswitches. No data about your device or surroundings is collected.
  • MIDI devices. Used to receive control messages and send MIDI clock to connected gear.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising.
  • No network connections. The app works fully offline.
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data.

Your data

Sessions you save are files in the app's container on your iPad. Deleting the app deletes them. You can also delete sessions from inside the app.
