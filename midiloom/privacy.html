MidiLoom Privacy Policy

Nothing leaves your iPad unless you ask the AI

SupportPrivacy PolicyEtherealSoul

Last updated: November 2, 2026

MidiLoom does not collect personal data. One optional feature, Ask AI for it, sends what you ask for to MidiLoom's server so it can design a graph; everything else stays on your iPad.

What the app accesses

  • Bluetooth. Used only to find and connect Bluetooth MIDI devices. No data about your device or surroundings is collected.
  • MIDI devices and apps. MidiLoom sends and receives MIDI with the devices and apps you connect. Nothing is recorded unless you add a Record node, and recordings stay on your iPad.
  • Audio output. The built-in Synth plays through your iPad's speakers, headphones or audio interface. MidiLoom does not use the microphone.

Ask AI for it

When you use Ask AI for it, your request, the graph you are changing (if any) and the names of your connected MIDI devices are sent to MidiLoom's server and on to Anthropic's Claude, which designs the graph. MidiLoom's server does not keep them. Anthropic does not use them to train its models and deletes them within 30 days, except where its usage policy requires otherwise; see Anthropic's privacy policy. MidiLoom's server runs on Cloudflare and does not log your internet address.

To keep track of your generations, the app makes a random account ID that stays on your iPad and is not linked to your name, email or Apple ID. The server stores that ID with your generation count and the purchases you redeemed (their App Store transaction numbers). Apple's App Attest lets the server check that requests come from MidiLoom; it identifies the app, not you.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising.
  • No network connections except Ask AI for it and the App Store. Everything else works offline; links in its Help open in Safari.
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data.

Your data

Graphs, settings, MIDI files and recordings are kept in the app's own storage on your iPad, which you can see in the Files app. Deleting the app deletes them.
