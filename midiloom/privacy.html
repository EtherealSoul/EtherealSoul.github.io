MidiLoom Privacy Policy

Nothing leaves your iPad

SupportPrivacy PolicyEtherealSoul

Last updated: October 3, 2026

MidiLoom does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

What the app accesses

  • Bluetooth. Used only to find and connect Bluetooth MIDI devices. No data about your device or surroundings is collected.
  • MIDI devices and apps. MidiLoom sends and receives MIDI with the devices and apps you connect. Nothing is recorded unless you add a Record node, and recordings stay on your iPad.
  • Audio output. The built-in Synth plays through your iPad's speakers, headphones or audio interface. MidiLoom does not use the microphone.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising.
  • No network connections. The app works fully offline; links in its Help open in Safari.
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data.

Your data

Graphs, settings, MIDI files and recordings are kept in the app's own storage on your iPad, which you can see in the Files app. Deleting the app deletes them.
